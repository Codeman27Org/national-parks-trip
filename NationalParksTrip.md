**Vehicle:** Tesla Model 3 (~260 mi range) · **Total:** ~1,000 mi · Battery % values below are planning estimates, so go by the car's trip planner on the day.

> ⚠️ **Range changes from the original plan**
> 1. **Day 1:** Swapped the Green River charge for the **Moab Supercharger**. Green River is ~20 mi past your turnoff, while Moab is only ~10 mi off route, and you take the scenic **UT-128 River Road** to get there. Skipping charging altogether on Day 1 would leave you at ~5–10% when you reach Green River on Day 2, and close to 0% with Camp Mode.
> 2. **Day 2:** Added a **Green River Supercharger** stop. Lone Mesa → Capitol Reef is ~145 mi, and you'd be starting at about 50%. Green River is right on the way.
> 3. **Day 2 → 3:** There's **no fast charging** between Green River and Bryce. You need to **leave Torrey at 85%+** to make it over Hwy 12/Boulder Mountain, so the Torrey charge is mandatory. The Torrey Supercharger is only *planned* as of 10/1/2026.
> 4. **Day 3:** Moved the Ruby's Inn charge to **lunchtime**. You'll arrive at about 30%, so it shouldn't wait until 4:30 PM.
> 5. **Day 4:** There's **no Supercharger in Hurricane**. The closest is **Toquerville**. I also swapped the camp from **Smithsonian Butte** to **Hurricane Cliffs** because legal Smithsonian sites need high-clearance 4x4 and there's active ticketing.
> 6. **Day 5:** Zion → Green River is **~270 mi**, which is over your range and ~4 hrs, not 2.5. Added **Toquerville → Richfield → Green River** charging. Expect to get home around **4 PM**.
